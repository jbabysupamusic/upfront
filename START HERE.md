# UPFRONT — Start Here

## 1. Get the installer (one time, ~10 minutes, free)
1. Sign in at **github.com** (free account) → **+** (top right) → **New repository**
   → name it `upfront` → **Create repository**.
2. Click **uploading an existing file**, drag in **everything in this folder**
   (including the `.github` folder*), click **Commit changes**.
3. Wait ~10 minutes. Then open **Releases** (right side of your repo page) and
   download **UPFRONT-Setup.exe**.

\* If the `.github` folder doesn't upload by dragging, turn on "Show hidden items"
in File Explorer (View menu), or drag the whole unzipped folder in at once.

Every time you upload changes, a fresh installer appears automatically.

## 2. Install
Double-click **UPFRONT-Setup.exe** → Next → Install. Done.

## 3. Use it in FL Studio
1. **Options → Manage plugins → Find installed plugins** (first time only).
2. Mixer insert → empty slot → **UPFRONT**.
3. Click **ANALYZE**, play your sound → click **BALANCE**. That's it.

---
*Have Visual Studio 2022 on your PC? Instead of step 1 you can double-click
**Build and Install (Windows).bat**.* Full manual: **README.md**.
